# FIPAT permission query — TA2 identifiers and terms

- **Status:** drafted 2026-10-06, not sent
- **Why:** Terminologia Anatomica (TA2) is CC BY-ND 4.0. FIPAT says individual terms are
  public domain, but translations and works "that might be considered derivative" need its
  permission. See the design spec §29.4.
- **Send to:** the Chair of FIPAT. Contact details are on the FIPAT TA2 page:
  <https://libraries.dal.ca/Fipat/ta2.html>

Before sending, check point 3: it assumes the Polish names come from an existing
nomenclature, and sourcing them is still an open task.

---

**Subject:** Permission query: use of TA2 identifiers and terms in an educational app

Dear Chair of FIPAT,

I am developing Anatomy Pro, a mobile anatomy atlas and quiz application for medical
students. I would like to confirm that my intended use of Terminologia Anatomica, Second
Edition, is consistent with its CC BY-ND 4.0 licence, or to request permission where it is
not.

The app would:

1. Use TA2 identifiers as stable internal keys for anatomical structures.
2. Display the official Latin terms and English equivalents for those structures, attributed
   to FIPAT/IFAA.
3. Display Polish equivalents alongside them. These would be taken from established Polish
   anatomical nomenclature, not newly translated from TA2 by me.
4. Store the above in a database inside the app, covering roughly 3,700 structures. The TA2
   documents themselves would not be reproduced or redistributed.

The app is commercial: browsing the atlas is free, and quiz features are offered by
subscription.

Could you tell me whether this use requires FIPAT's permission as a derivative work, and if
so, how I should apply for it? I would also be glad to follow whatever attribution wording
you prefer.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
Bartosz Piątek
